What MahalaX is

The token that powers the FORUS economy.

MahalaX (MHX) is the utility and rewards token of the FORUS network. MahalaX holders were the early backers who funded years of FORUS development. Now that the platform is live, MHX is the incentive layer that rewards activity across it.

1

Earned through real activity

MHX is issued when real things happen on the network: payments, signups, merchant activity and campaigns. Every qualifying payment in FORUS Connect can earn MHX rewards.

2

Built for use

MHX is made to be used inside the FORUS ecosystem: spent with merchants, earned as rewards, entered into draws and given to partner charities through GoodFORUS.

3

Limited by design

The policy supply is 360 million MHX. New MHX enters circulation only through approved trust lines on the XRP Ledger, with every issuance recorded on-ledger.

How it works

Every transaction funds the network. At source.

When you make a mobile call, the network checks your SIM and balance before it connects you. MHX applies the same idea to money. Every transaction on FORUS is split into five pools at the moment it is authorised, inside the FORUS Rail Connector, before it settles. No month-end sweeps. No reconciliation gap. No unfunded promises.

Authorise

Identity, trust line and compliance are checked before anything moves.

Compute the split

The routing engine works out the five-way split on the net amount, before settlement.

Settle together

Each pool's share lands in its own wallet in the same settlement instruction.

Issue MHX

The rewards share triggers MHX issuance on the XRP Ledger: issuer, to treasury, to member. Fully logged.

Fund the draws

The sweepstake share lands in a ring-fenced prize wallet, so prizes are funded before they are promised.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Why does my wallet show 100 times more MHX?

MahalaX was redenominated 100× when it moved to the XRP Ledger. Every balance was multiplied by 100 and the total supply went from 3.6 million to 360 million. Your share of the supply is exactly the same.

Do I need Xaman or another wallet app?

No. Your MahalaX wallet is created and managed inside FORUS Connect. FORUS manages recovery and sponsors activation.

Do I have to pay anything to claim?

No. There is no fee to create your wallet, submit your claim or unlock your allocation. Anyone asking you to pay to release MHX is not FORUS.

When will my MHX arrive?

After an independent administrator approves your claim, 10% of your allocation is delivered first. Then 7.5% of your original allocation arrives every month for 12 months. Your wallet's on-ledger balance shows confirmed deliveries.

Submitting a claim, creating a wallet and approval are separate steps from delivery. Follow the status in the app.

My wallet says “Platform activation is required”. What do I do?

Keep that wallet while FORUS completes its setup, then tap Refresh saved wallet and claim status. Do not create a second wallet or submit another claim.

The email, amount or wallet looks wrong.

Stop and contact your usual trusted FORUS administrator before you continue. Do not submit a duplicate claim.

What is MHX worth?

The FORUS reference value is US$1 per MHX. It is the price advertisers pay and the advertising value each MHX buys. Real value grows with use, liquidity and confidence across the network.

Where do I get the FORUS Connect app?

FORUS Connect is free on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. Search for FORUS Connect, or use the buttons on this page, and check the publisher is FORUS before you install.

Is MahalaX part of FORUS?

Yes. MahalaX is the token layer of the FORUS network, and FORUS Connect is where you hold, earn and use it.
