What MahalaX is

The token that powers the FORUS economy.

MahalaX (MHX) is the utility and rewards token of the FORUS network. MahalaX holders were the early backers who funded years of FORUS development. Now that the platform is live, MHX is the incentive layer that rewards activity across it.

1

Earned through real activity

MHX is issued when real things happen on the network: payments, signups, merchant activity and campaigns. Every qualifying payment in FORUS Connect can earn MHX rewards.

2

Built for use

MHX is made to be used inside the FORUS ecosystem: spent with merchants, earned as rewards, entered into draws and given to partner charities through GoodFORUS.

3

Limited by design

The policy supply is 360 million MHX. New MHX enters circulation only through approved trust lines on the XRP Ledger, with every issuance recorded on-ledger.

How it works

Every transaction funds the network. At source.

When you make a mobile call, the network checks your SIM and balance before it connects you. MHX applies the same idea to money. Every transaction on FORUS is split into five pools at the moment it is authorised, inside the FORUS Rail Connector, before it settles. No month-end sweeps. No reconciliation gap. No unfunded promises.

Authorise

Identity, trust line and compliance are checked before anything moves.

Compute the split

The routing engine works out the five-way split on the net amount, before settlement.

Settle together

Each pool's share lands in its own wallet in the same settlement instruction.

Issue MHX

The rewards share triggers MHX issuance on the XRP Ledger: issuer, to treasury, to member. Fully logged.

Fund the draws

The sweepstake share lands in a ring-fenced prize wallet, so prizes are funded before they are promised.

Liquidity and cash redemption

An orderly path to liquidity.

Cash redemption is designed as a release valve, not the main way to use MHX. It grows with real adoption and is paced to protect every holder.

Programme in preparation. No cash window is open yet. Pricing, eligibility, fees and the share of surplus are approved before any window opens, and each window is announced here first.

How windows are designed to work

Months 1 to 3

Verification hold

Complete identity checks, open your MahalaX wallet and acknowledge the terms.

Month 4

First window

Verified holders may request up to 5% of their unlocked balance.

Every quarter

Windows repeat

Months 7, 10, 13 and on. Unfilled requests roll forward to the next window.

Ongoing

The pool grows

The liquidity share of every transaction keeps building the reserve.

Funded first

Only cleared, earned operating cash left after delivery obligations, tax, fees, refunds, prizes, costs and a cash buffer can fund a window. No surplus means no new window.

Fair when busy

If requests are larger than the window, everyone receives the same share of what they asked for. 25% of the pool is always held back as a safety reserve.

Always your choice

Selling is voluntary. Each window publishes its price, cash cap, dates, eligibility, fees and allocation method. Unsold MHX stays yours.

Backing the token

The 10% liquidity share is divided two ways.

60% XRPL market
40% cash float

On-ledger depth lets members and merchants exchange MHX inside the ecosystem at reliable prices. The off-ledger cash float means a merchant who accepts MHX always has a funded way to cash out.

Kept in balance

The policy floor is 30% liquidity cover of outstanding MHX value. The base case reaches 62.5% by month 36. If cover trends toward the floor, two levers respond: a larger liquidity share of each transaction, or slower MHX issuance.

Protections and transparency

Built to be checked.

Every MHX issuance and pool movement is logged in the Rail Connector and on the XRP Ledger, where anyone can verify it at any time.

Approved trust lines only

MHX is issued only through authorised trust lines on the XRP Ledger. No free minting and no off-ledger copies.

Multi-approval wallets

Issuer, treasury and campaign wallets are kept separate, each needing several sign-offs, with approval logs. The redemption reserve is ring-fenced from operating accounts.

Independent review

An independent administrator reviews every holder claim before delivery. Identity is verified before any cash redemption.

Full audit trail

Every issuance, delivery and pool movement is recorded, both in the FORUS platform and on the public ledger.

Regulated approach

FORUS reviews South African FSCA requirements for all applicable activities, and draw mechanics are approved per country before launch.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Why does my wallet show 100 times more MHX?

MahalaX was redenominated 100× when it moved to the XRP Ledger. Every balance was multiplied by 100 and the total supply went from 3.6 million to 360 million. Your share of the supply is exactly the same.

Do I need Xaman or another wallet app?

No. Your MahalaX wallet is created and managed inside FORUS Connect. FORUS manages recovery and sponsors activation.

Do I have to pay anything to claim?

No. There is no fee to create your wallet, submit your claim or unlock your allocation. Anyone asking you to pay to release MHX is not FORUS.

When will my MHX arrive?

After an independent administrator approves your claim, 10% of your allocation is delivered first. Then 7.5% of your original allocation arrives every month for 12 months. Your wallet's on-ledger balance shows confirmed deliveries.

Submitting a claim, creating a wallet and approval are separate steps from delivery. Follow the status in the app.

My wallet says “Platform activation is required”. What do I do?

Keep that wallet while FORUS completes its setup, then tap Refresh saved wallet and claim status. Do not create a second wallet or submit another claim.

The email, amount or wallet looks wrong.

Stop and contact your usual trusted FORUS administrator before you continue. Do not submit a duplicate claim.

Can I cash out my MHX?

Not yet. The cash redemption programme is in preparation. When windows open, they will be quarterly, voluntary and funded from earned surplus, with up to 5% of your unlocked balance per window. Spending MHX in the ecosystem is the main way to use it.

What is MHX worth?

The FORUS reference value is US$1 per MHX. It is the price advertisers pay, the advertising value each MHX buys and the proposed sale price in a funded window. Real value grows with use, liquidity and confidence across the network.

I use an iPhone. How do I get the app?

Install Apple's TestFlight from the App Store, then open the FORUS Connect beta link in Safari on your iPhone. The beta connects to the live ledger and uses real MHX. TestFlight builds expire, so update when asked.

Is MahalaX part of FORUS?

Yes. MahalaX is the token layer of the FORUS network, and FORUS Connect is where you hold, earn and use it.
