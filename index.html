Example

A simple LuckyX campaign.

Here is how MHX moves between a merchant and their customers in one campaign.

Step 1 · Merchant

Rewards customers with MHX

The merchant acquires MHX through the merchant portal and uses it to reward customers in a LuckyX campaign.

Step 2 · Customer

Uses MHX for rewards

Customers who receive MHX use it to get the merchant rewards offered through the campaign.

Step 3 · Merchant

Exchanges rewards for MHX

The merchant exchanges the rewards they receive through the campaign for MHX, ready for the next campaign.

And the cycle continues

i

Every campaign sets its own rules: how participants receive MHX, which rewards they can get and how exchanges work.
